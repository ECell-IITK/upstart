About UpStart

Nurturing early-stage ventures into market leaders through mentorship, incubation, and investor networking.

An event that caters to the budding startups of our country, UpStart – The Indian Business Model Competition stands apart from conventional competitions. We strive to give our participants more than just prize money.

Startups go through multiple rounds of rigorous screening, structured brainstorming sessions at different levels, and 1-on-1 face-to-face mentorship with seasoned industry leaders.

Finalists get to hone their business models alongside a dedicated panel of VCs and domain experts, accelerating their venture growth on a national stage.

Judging Criteria

Every entry in UpStart is rigorously evaluated by VC panels based on these core parameters:

  • Originality of Idea and innovative value proposition.
  • Scalability in terms of growth potential and venture replication.
  • Well-conceived implementation plan and realistic execution timetable.
  • Overall likelihood of achieving demonstrable market results.

Official Status

Timeline Criteria

Founder Ownership
